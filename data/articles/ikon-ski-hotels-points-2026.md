Ski lodging on points looks nothing like it did a few seasons ago.

For 2026–27, Ikon covers **77 destinations on five continents**, spring pricing started at $1,349 for the full pass and $924 for Base, and almost every hotel program in this guide has repriced ski-season awards in the last two seasons. Marriott, Hilton and IHG have no award chart at all. Hyatt kept a chart, then widened it dramatically in May 2026. Wyndham kept a fixed chart, then added a new top tier in September 2026. Choice quietly became the most interesting transfer play in the mountains.

This guide covers six programs, **World of Hyatt, Marriott Bonvoy, Hilton Honors, IHG One Rewards, Choice Privileges and Wyndham Rewards**. No other loyalty programs, and no independent lodges, however good they are. If a resort has no useful option in those six, we say so plainly.

> **The Loyalist angle:** A points price tells you what a night costs. It doesn't tell you what your status gets you at 7 a.m. on a powder day, breakfast before first lift, a waived resort fee, free parking, a late checkout that matches the last chair, or a suite upgrade that actually clears. Throughout this guide we flag the status perk that matters *for skiing*, not just in general. Been on one of these trips? Report your stay on Loyalist Travel, what you booked, what you got, and whether status moved the needle is exactly the data this guide is built to collect.

### How to Read the Points Ranges

- **Standard rooms only**, one night, winter season, unless a property is priced per bedroom (Wyndham vacation clubs and Vacasa, flagged every time).
- Ranges are **indicative for 2026–27, checked October 2026, not quotes**. Dynamic programs (Marriott, Hilton, IHG, Choice) move with cash prices daily. Refundable bookings can be repriced as prices move.
- Cash ranges, where given, are typical mid-winter ranges before taxes/fees, meant for value math, not a promise for Christmas week or Presidents' Weekend, when everything in this guide can double.

## The Slope-Side Shortlist: Best in Each Program

If you read nothing else, read this. These are the stays where points still beat cash by a mile in 2026:

- **Hyatt, Grand Hyatt Deer Valley (UT).** Category 6, so 20,000–40,000 points under the new chart, against cash that ran $600–$1,000+ in Jan–Feb and around $900 at peak when it opened. Ski access to Deer Valley's expanded East Village terrain, huge family footprint, and Globalist breakfast. It is the standout points opening of the last two seasons.
- **Marriott, The St. Regis Deer Valley (UT).** Slope-side, ski valet, funicular to Main Street. Recent ski-season pricing from ~75,200 points, running to ~138,000 on peak dates, against cash approaching $4,000 on the biggest nights. Painful, and still the math works. Use an 85k certificate + top-up on a shoulder night.
- **Hilton, Waldorf Astoria Park City (UT).** Standard rooms at 110,000 points this ski season, next to the Canyons gondola. The natural home for an Aspire certificate + 5th-night-free combo.
- **Wyndham, Club Wyndham / Vacasa condos in Park City, Steamboat and Breckenridge.** At the unchanged 15,000-points-per-bedroom tier, a one-bedroom near the gondola for 15,000 points with fees waived on Vacasa bookings is still the cheapest slope-side bed in this entire guide. Book early, availability, not price, is the constraint, and minimum stays apply.
- **IHG, Kimpton Taconic, Manchester, VT (for Stratton).** IHG's only genuinely charming ski-town option in the East, supercharged by 4th-night-free. In the West, IHG is a Holiday Inn Express value play, which, at $300+ cash in ski towns, is not nothing.
- **Choice, Cambria Hotel Copper Mountain (CO).** A 127-room Cambria that opened in 2023 *at* Copper, steps from the lifts, Choice finally has a ski-in-adjacent flagship in the US, and Preferred Hotels opens up alpine properties in Europe that Choice's own brands never reached.

### Winter Park & Eldora

Winter Park is 89 miles from DEN, and the **Winter Park Express train from Denver Union Station** remains the best no-drive ski commute in America.

### Alta, Snowbird, Brighton, Solitude & Snowbasin

There is **still no slope-side hotel from our six programs at Alta, Snowbird, Brighton or Solitude**, on-mountain lodging there is independent lodges. The play is the Cottonwood corridor in Salt Lake's southeast suburbs (~25–35 min, canyon traffic permitting):

- **Hyatt Place Salt Lake City / Cottonwood (Hyatt)**, the closest Hyatt to both canyons, chart-priced well below Park City Hyatts.
- **Residence Inn Cottonwood + Courtyard / SpringHill options (Marriott)**, **Hampton Inn Cottonwood + Homewood Suites (Hilton)**, **Holiday Inn Express Midvale (IHG)**, **Comfort Inn / Quality Inn corridor (Choice)** and **Hawthorn Suites Midvale area (Wyndham, fixed tier)**, pick by who gives you breakfast earliest and parking free; all are a canyon drive, none is a resort, and in a storm cycle that's exactly what you want: cheap, flexible, cancellable.

### Big Sky (MT)

BZN is ~52 miles away, rent the car, watch the Gallatin Canyon in weather.

- **The Wilson Hotel, Residence Inn Big Sky (Marriott)**, still the only big-six points hotel in Big Sky town center, ~12 min to the base. Studios/suites, free hot breakfast, outdoor pool/whirlpool. Dynamic pricing now swings hard on winter weekends, budget well above shoulder-season rates, and use 5th-night-free.
- Everything else is Bozeman (all six programs represented, cheapest at IHG/Choice/Wyndham), a fine flight-night, a poor daily ski commute in a storm.

### Sun Valley & Schweitzer (ID)

- **Sun Valley:** the resort lodges are independent. Base in Hailey / Ketchum value motels (Wyndham-branded options appear at fixed tiers) or accept Boise (2.5 hrs) as a flight base only. This is an Ikon gap in all six programs, say so to readers rather than padding it.
- **Schweitzer:** base in Sandpoint (~20 min), **La Quinta by Wyndham Sandpoint** at a fixed tier, plus Choice options in Sandpoint/Ponderay. Spokane (GEG, ~80 miles) has every program if you're combining with a travel day.

### Crystal Mountain, The Summit at Snoqualmie (WA) & Mt. Bachelor (OR)

- **Crystal / Summit:** still no big-six hotel within ~25 miles of either. Closest useful: **Hampton Inn & Suites Snoqualmie (Hilton)** for the Summit (~30 min), Enumclaw/Issaquah corridor hotels (IHG, Choice, Wyndham) for Crystal, or Seattle/Tacoma suburbs as a dual-purpose base. Book cancellable, I-90 pass closures decide your ski day more than your hotel does.
- **Mt. Bachelor:** Bend is the best ski town in this guide for points depth, **Sunriver Resort area (Destination by Hyatt properties surface there intermittently), Hilton Garden Inn / DoubleTree Bend (Hilton), SpringHill Suites / Residence Inn Bend (Marriott), Holiday Inn Express Bend (IHG), plus Choice and Wyndham** across Bend/Redmond at prices Park City would envy. ~25 min to Bachelor.

### Alyeska (AK)

Girdwood's Alyeska Resort hotel is independent (and the mountain is the trip). Anchorage (ANC, ~40 min) has all six programs, **Hotel Captain Cook is not one of them; use Hilton Anchorage, Marriott Anchorage Downtown, Hyatt Place Anchorage, Holiday Inn Express Anchorage, or Choice/Wyndham along the Seward Highway corridor**. Treat Anchorage as night 1, Girdwood (book cash, book early) as ski nights.

### Michigan, Minnesota & Wisconsin (Including the 2026 Newcomers)

- **The Highlands & Boyne Mountain (MI):** Petoskey (20–30 min) is the base, Courtyard Petoskey (Marriott), Holiday Inn Express Petoskey (IHG), Hampton Inn Petoskey (Hilton), Quality Inn Petoskey (Choice), Days Inn / AmericInn Petoskey (Wyndham, fixed).

- **NEW, Granite Peak (WI):** Wausau is a full-chain town 10 minutes away, Hilton Garden Inn, Hampton, Fairfield, Holiday Inn Express, La Quinta and Comfort Inn all compete, all cheap by ski-country standards. The Midwest additions are where your Choice/Wyndham balances suddenly go furthest.

### Pennsylvania: Camelback & Blue Mountain (PA)

A gift for East Coast points holders: Stroudsburg / Tannersville / Allentown corridor has **Hampton and Homewood (Hilton), Holiday Inn Express (IHG), Fairfield (Marriott), Comfort / Quality (Choice) and Wyndham motels** within 15–35 minutes, at Poconos prices. Camelback's own lodge is independent/aquatic-resort, fun, cash, not points.

### West Virginia: Snowshoe

Still no big-six hotel within an hour. ### Taos Ski Valley (NM)

Taos town is 19 miles / ~35 min below the valley, stay there, eat better, pay less:

**Hotel Don Fernando de Taos, Tapestry by Hilton** and **Hampton Inn Taos (Hilton)**, **Quality Inn Taos (Choice)**, **Days Inn / Super 8 Taos (Wyndham, fixed)**, plus the **Sagebrush / El Monte** independents outside scope. Don Fernando is the nicest big-six room in town; the Wyndhams are the cheapest predictable ski beds in New Mexico. Hotel and ski shuttle availability varies by season, so the rental car is the safe default.

|---|---|---|---|

## How to Stock Up: 2026 Edition

| Program | Best transfer move in 2026 | Watch out for |
|---|---|---|
| Hyatt | Bilt (1:1) and Chase, but note Sapphire Preferred / Ink Preferred drop to **4:3 from Oct 1, 2026**; Sapphire Reserve holds 1:1. If you're sitting on Preferred points earmarked for Niseko or Deer Valley, the transfer deadline was real. | Category 1–7 certificates no longer reach Category 8 ski icons at any band |
| Marriott | Amex/Chase transfers are usually poor value, earn via stays, cards and bought-points sales only when a specific dynamic price is mispriced low | Resort fees on awards; certificates cap + 15k top-up only |
| Hilton | **Amex 1:2** is the engine. Frequent 40–50% transfer bonuses make Waldorf Park City cost ~55k Amex points a night in standard terms | Premium rooms are not standard rooms, filter for "standard room reward" before falling in love with a date |
| IHG | Chase/Bilt transfers for a specific stay; **buy-points sales at ~0.5¢** are often rational for ski towns where cash is $250+ and awards price near cash × 0.5¢ | Dynamic pricing means the 4th-night-free is doing most of the work, on a 2-night trip, compare cash first |
| Choice | **Wells Fargo 1:2** is the best headline ratio left in hotel points; Citi is now 1:1.5 (was 1:2), Amex and Capital One 1:1 | No free-night multiplier. The value is Preferred Hotels and cheap US midscale, price both before transferring |
| Wyndham | Capital One and other bank transfers at modest ratios; co-branded Earner cards for the redemption discount | **Sept 15, 2026 reshuffle moved individual hotels between tiers**, a hotel that was 15k last season may be 30k (or 45k) now, so the specific property's current tier is what matters |

**The universal ski-trip rules, 2026 edition:**

1. **Book points the day you buy the pass.** Slope-side award inventory (Grand Hyatt Deer Valley, Club Wyndham Park City, Park Hyatt Niseko, Sheraton Steamboat) is a spring/early-summer game, not a December game.
2. **Use the multiplier, not the headline.** A 60k Marriott night is 48k effective on a 5-night stay. A 50k IHG night is 37.5k effective on a 4-night cardholder stay. Compare effective prices.
3. **Reprice dynamic awards monthly.** Marriott/Hilton/IHG/Choice prices fall when cash falls (shoulder-season warm spells are your friend). Cancel-and-rebook is free money.
4. **Fixed-chart programs are your powder-weekend hedge.** When a storm cycle spikes every dynamic price in Utah, Wyndham's fixed tiers and Hyatt's capped chart bands (if you can find Low/Moderate) are the ceiling the other programs don't have.
5. **Always do the resort-fee math.** A "cheaper" Marriott award that still charges $40/night resort fee + $35 parking can lose to a Hyatt award that waives both, especially for Globalists.

## Bottom Line

Booking ski hotels on points used to be a spreadsheet exercise: look up a fixed price, transfer, done.

In 2026 it's a strategy. Hyatt still offers the best *capped* value in skiing, Grand Hyatt Deer Valley at Category 6 is the redemption of the year, and Park Hyatt Niseko is worth its new Category 8 price on a standard-band night and not on a Top-band night. Marriott offers the best slope-side *footprint* (Snowmass, Steamboat, Deer Valley, Tremblant, Blue Mountain, Mammoth) if you'll play the dynamic-pricing and 5th-night game. Hilton is the easiest balance to build and now owns the boutique back door (SLH) in the Alps and Jackson. IHG is the 4th-night-free value play in town after town. Choice, with a hotel *at* Copper, a 1:2 Wells Fargo transfer, and Preferred Hotels, is no longer the punchline of ski-points guides. And Wyndham, even after its 45,000-point top tier arrived, still sells the cheapest predictable condo by the gondola in America, if you book it before everyone else reading this does.

Where the pass went new for 2026–27, SilverStar, the Midwest trio, Madarao, points are thin, cheap and mostly Choice/Wyndham/IHG. That's not a flaw in the guide. That's the opportunity: those are the trips your leftover balances were made for.
